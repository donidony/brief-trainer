      Garden plots, staircases and shared entrances

      Multiple slots can share a street approach. A registered street-access point does not verify the mailbox location, a garden entrance, parking permission or a walking connection. Amber notes show approaches more than 30 metres from the registered access point; those distances are straight-line offsets, not measured walking distances.

      Some suffixes and collective address ranges are not separately registered. Their notes explain which parent building or shared approach is used. Do not treat these as independently verified entrances.

      Using the GPX on your phone

      Download the full GPX track and open it in a navigation app that supports following an imported track. Keep the saved track rather than recalculating it with a bicycle profile. Whether voice guidance and background GPS work depends on the app. The GPX includes the complete computed road line and numbered approach waypoints.

      The online directions are split into sections because the public moped routing service accepts at most 10 locations per request. Section boundaries overlap by one approach point.

      How this route was calculated

      The street order was estimated on a directed OpenStreetMap road graph with the start and finish fixed, then every leg was calculated with Valhalla’s motor_scooter profile at 45 km/h. It respects the map’s one-way and moped-access data. Mapping errors, unrecorded restrictions and temporary closures can still occur; current signs take precedence.

      Registered mopeds generally cannot use bicycle-only paths or bicycle-only one-way exceptions. Austrian transport ministry rules. This draft assumes no special access permit.
